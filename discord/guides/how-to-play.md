# 🥷 How to play Hello Bro
A short guide to the game on the revival servers. Not installed yet? See <#1565697833150058507>. Questions? Ask in <#1565697834571927694>.
📖 **Wiki:** every card, gear, ninjutsu, seal and stage, and where to get it: https://ninjaonlinereborn.com/wiki/

## 1. Energy and grudge
• **Energy** (red bar) is used for training. 1 point comes back every 3 minutes, a level-up refills it, and it refills to full every day at 22:00. Out of energy? Eat ramen.
• **Free ramen:** between **12:00-14:00** and again **18:00-20:00**, tap the **Noodle** stall on Home (scroll right) for full energy and grudge. (Its sign says "0:00~24:00", ignore that.)
• **Grudge** (crossed swords) is used for Spar. 1 point comes back every 2 hours. A **Hyourou-gan** refills half (Small) or all (Big) of it.
• Ramen and Hyourou-gan can be used 20 and 30 times a day, a few more per VIP level.

## 2. Training
• Story stages give EXP and silver. Each stage needs a few clears before the next one opens.
• **Bosses** have 3 free tries a day, then 10 gold a try. Every boss brings its own enemies with the same strength as in the original game: lost? Make your team stronger and come back. Every boss also has a **5% chance** to drop the special card shown on its row (Graduate II = Kakashi Hatake).
• From VIP 1 and level 80, **Save time** (the EXP clock on Home) turns a Big Ramen into EXP and silver.
• **Finished the story?** Passed stages show **Finish** and can't be played again (same as the original game). But every boss has its **own** 3 free tries a day (165 bosses in total), so you can go back to any part and fight its boss again. Energy is the real limit.

## 3. Your team and elements
• More team slots open as you level up (level 2, 6, 15, 30 ...) and clear story stages.
• Every card has an **element**: Water beats Fire, Fire beats Wind, Wind beats Thunder, Thunder beats Earth, Earth beats Water. Hitting the weak element does **15% more** ("Restrain" on the result screen).
• Give each ninja a weapon, armor, trinket, seal and ninjutsu. Gear makes the biggest difference.
• From level 100 the **Team 2** tab opens: build a second line-up (it may use the same cards) and press **Activate** to fight with it.

## 4. Getting cards and gear
• **Recruit** (Shop): the blue recruit is free every 2 days, the purple one every 3 days (like the original). Every 10th purple pull without a 5-star card is a sure 5-star (the window counts down: "Recruit N more will get one").
• Shop gift bags give gear, and their description tells you the stars (a 3-star bag gives 2 to 4 stars, a 4-star bag 3 to 5).
• **Advance** in the Base upgrades 5-star level-50 gear (50 000 silver).

## 5. Fighting other players
• **Spar** (bottom bar): win EXP, silver and war achievements.
• **Arena** (Home, from level 25): climb the ranks for daily silver and reputation. 25 challenges a day (+1 per VIP level); after every 3 there is a 10-minute wait, or pay 30 gold to skip it. Claim the daily reward with the **Claim** button on your own row in the Compete list (once a day, until midnight). Ranks 1-1000 also get a **ladder prize** with it (gear, a seal, gold or silver: see the Arena Rules); it goes to your backpack. Spend reputation in the **Exchange** tab (some items once ever, pills twice a day).
• **Battles** (Home): 25 chapters of 7 rounds, the 7th is the chapter boss. **Ranking > Achievement** pays a reward every 7 floors.
• **News**: the Battle tab shows who sparred or plundered you (press **Revenge** to fight back), the Friend tab shows messages from friends.
• **War** and **Border war**: fight for your nation. War is open all day; the clock shows the time until midnight. After every 3 attacks you wait 10 minutes (or pay 30 gold). **Autoplay** (50 gold) attacks for you for the rest of the day, even when you are offline. National treasure: 1 hour between raids (15 gold skips it).
• Real players fight with their **whole team**. Bots bring about as many cards as you do, and every enemy card's stats vary a little each fight. Watching a fight or pressing **Skip** always gives the same result.

## 6. Cross-server battle
• Find the **Cross** building on Home (scroll the map to the right).
• **Until Friday 8 pm:** buy a ticket for 500 gold. The arena top 20 get in for free.
• **Saturday 8-9 pm:** challenge the players above you on the ladder. Every position earns points each second, and higher positions earn more.
• **Inspire:** 200 gold for +10% attack and defence for 5 minutes.
• **Merit tab:** rankings and the **point shop** (24 000 to 850 000 points). Points carry over to the next week.

## 7. Base buildings
• **Advance**: upgrade gear (see chapter 4).
• **Ninja Test** (level 45): 15 stages a day with your 4 and 5-star ninjas. Stages 5, 10 and 15 have boss chests (free, 160 and 250 gold).
• **Breakthrough** for God cards, and **Pets** from level 150.

## 8. Events and VIP
• Check the **Event** menu and the **Daily Task** list every day for free rewards.
• **Festivals** (when switched on): win fights for festival items (Christmas Caps and Trees, or Fireworks and Balloons for the Anniversary) and trade them in the Event menu for rare cards.
• **Super Ninja** (Home): every hour a new god card is the prize. Gold draw 300 (10 for 2800) gives 4-5 star cards, every 10th draw is a sure 5-star, and you get 1 free draw a day. Every draw is 10 points; **every hour** 1st place wins that god card, 2nd-3rd get 300 gold, 4th-10th 100 gold (you need at least 50 points). When the hour ends the next card starts right away and the prizes arrive in your **Awards** chest.
• **World Boss** (Event menu): an Akatsuki member invades every day from **14:00 to 14:30**: 10 hits, then a short wait. Hit it for reputation and spend that in its shop. A new boss comes after each one falls (Kakuzu, Sasori, Deidara, Hidan ...).
• **Ninja Treasure** (chest on Home, left side): Explore for gold to win the 9 prizes on the board (35, 70, 300 ... gold, rising). Every board hides one big card from **Top Rewards**; a new board comes every 2 hours, or press Refresh (100 gold).
• **Awards** chest (Home, right side): gifts the server owner sends you and event prizes (Super Ninja). Press **Claim**; they stay 7 days.
• **Sign-in** (Event menu): log in on days in a row for ramen, Hyourou-gan and silver; from day 7 also 20 gold a day. Missing a day starts again at day 1.
• **Lottery** and **Wheel of Fortune** open at level 10.
• Sometimes switched on: **God of Wealth** (invest gold in 8 steps, always get a bit more back), **Flower Worship** (every dollar topped up gives a rose; give a goddess her roses for a rare card), **Month card** (a 4.99 or 9.99 top-up gives 60 or 120 gold a day for 30 days).
• Free top-ups (one a day) raise your VIP, up to **VIP 23**. Badges stop at VIP18, but your real level counts.
