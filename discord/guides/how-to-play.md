# 🥷 How to play Hello Bro
A short guide to the game on the revival servers. Not installed yet? See <#1565697833150058507>. Questions? Ask in <#1565697834571927694>.

## 1. Energy and grudge
• **Energy** (red bar) is used for training. 1 point comes back every 3 minutes, and a level-up refills it. Out of energy? Eat ramen.
• **Grudge** (crossed swords) is used for Spar. 1 point comes back every 2 hours.

## 2. Training
• Story stages give EXP and silver. Each stage needs a few clears before the next one opens.
• **Bosses** have 3 free tries a day, then 10 gold a try. They are hard on purpose: lost? Make your team stronger and come back.
• From VIP 1 and level 80, **Save time** (the EXP clock on Home) turns a Big Ramen into EXP and silver.

## 3. Your team and elements
• More team slots open as you level up (level 2, 6, 15, 30 ...) and clear story stages.
• Every card has an **element**: Water beats Fire, Fire beats Wind, Wind beats Thunder, Thunder beats Earth, Earth beats Water. Hitting the weak element does **15% more** ("Restrain" on the result screen).
• Give each ninja a weapon, armor, trinket, seal and ninjutsu. Gear makes the biggest difference.

## 4. Getting cards and gear
• **Recruit** (Shop): the blue recruit is free every 8 hours, the purple one every 24 hours. After 20 purple pulls a 5-star card is guaranteed.
• Shop gift bags give gear, and their description tells you the stars (a 3-star bag gives 2 to 4 stars, a 4-star bag 3 to 5).
• **Advance** in the Base upgrades 5-star level-50 gear (50 000 silver).

## 5. Fighting other players
• **Spar** (bottom bar): win EXP, silver and war achievements.
• **Arena** (Home, from level 25): climb the ranks for daily silver and reputation.
• **War** and **Border war**: fight for your nation.
• Real players fight with their **whole team**. Bots bring about as many cards as you do, and every enemy card's stats vary a little each fight. Watching a fight or pressing **Skip** always gives the same result.

## 6. Cross-server battle
• Find the **Cross** building on Home (scroll the map to the right).
• **Until Friday 8 pm:** buy a ticket for 500 gold. The arena top 20 get in for free.
• **Saturday 8-9 pm:** challenge the players above you on the ladder. Every position earns points each second, and higher positions earn more.
• **Inspire:** 200 gold for +10% attack and defence for 5 minutes.
• **Merit tab:** rankings and the **point shop** (24 000 to 850 000 points). Points carry over to the next week.

## 7. Base buildings
• **Advance**: upgrade gear (see chapter 4).
• **Ninja Test** (level 45): 15 stages a day with your 4 and 5-star ninjas. Stages 5, 10 and 15 have boss chests (free, 160 and 250 gold).
• **Breakthrough** for God cards, and **Pets** from level 150.

## 8. Events and VIP
• Check the **Event** menu and the **Daily Task** list every day for free rewards.
• **World Boss** (Event menu): an Akatsuki member invades every day. Hit it for reputation and spend that in its shop. A new boss comes after each one falls (Kakuzu, Sasori, Deidara, Hidan ...).
• **Lottery** and **Wheel of Fortune** open at level 10.
• Top-ups are free (one package a day) and raise your VIP level. VIP gives extra daily chances (Save time, gold chests and more).
