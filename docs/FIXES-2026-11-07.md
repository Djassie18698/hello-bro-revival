# Fixes of 7 November 2026

What changed on 7 November, in plain words. The owner asked to make training "as it was in the original game". Claude read
all 4 699 photos from the Facebook group with the Windows text reader, found the training screens among them, and compared
them with the game's own data tables.

**How it was checked:** "in game" = seen on the Claude emulator with a test server on its own port. "photos" = old screenshots
of the original game from the Facebook group.

---

## 1. Training (story)

| What | Detail | Checked |
|---|---|---|
| Bosses are the original ones | Every boss now brings **all** its own enemies (2 at the first boss, up to 15 at the end of the story), each with the strength the game's own table gives it. Seven victory photos show exactly these numbers: Kakuzu 21 162 defence, Hidan 19 474, Obito 31 425, Nagato 39 574, Sakon Ukon 9 643, Jiroubou 9 383, Night Kai 1 600 544. This replaces our own "steeper" curve of 6 November: from chapter 4 on, bosses are 2 to 15 times weaker than yesterday (end of chapter 10: 24 905 instead of 300 000), because that is how the original was. | photos + in game: Hidan, 9 of 9 enemies, 20 209 defence |
| Boss name on the result screen | The result screen showed "Enemy"; it now shows the boss's name, like the original ("Hidan", "Kakuzu"). | in game |
| Story wheel odds | The wheel after a normal stage now lands like the original, using the odds stored in the game's own table: silver x2 about 48 %, EXP x2 24 %, a card 24 %, "work harder" 5 %, gold almost never (we had 20 % for 3 gold). Some early stages have no EXP x2 at all (the table says so). | in game: silver x2 paid 468 |
| Wheel card | The card on the wheel is a 1-2 star shinobi, a seal fragment, 2 four-star card fragments or a bronze key (what the original pack held is lost, this is our choice, close to the pack's name "Shinobi Card, Seal Fragments, Advanced Card Fragments"). | test save |
| Already original | 3 free tries a day per boss and the Requirements / EXP / silver / Progress numbers on every stage match the photos. The boss retry price was 10, 15 or 20 gold in the original; it stays 10 for all (owner, 6 Nov). | photos |
