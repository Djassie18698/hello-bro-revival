# Fixes of 17 November 2026

What changed on 17 November, in plain words.

**How it was checked:** "in game" = seen on the Claude emulator with a test server on its own port. "live save" = a copy of the public
server's save. "Chrome" = the browser game in Chrome on the PC.

---

## 1. Team building: copies don't stack any more

| What | Detail | Checked |
|---|---|---|
| Copies of a card | Many god cards have a chain that gives **all Shinobi +X %** while the card is in the team. Every copy used to add it again, so the strongest teams were 21-23 copies of one card (22 x 18 %). Now each card's bonus counts **once**, however many copies are in the team. This is also the original game's rule (official Q&A, December 2014: "three Kakashi cards, 2 attack 1 defence, still give only two effects"). Owner's wish: "It looks dumb when they have 25 Sasukes in the team, and disables any kind of cool teambuilding." | live save: teams of copies drop to about a third, mixed teams 84-100 % |

## 2. Fixes

| What | Detail | Checked |
|---|---|---|
| Ninjutsu Bag | Buying a 4-star or 3-star Ninjutsu Bag (or the Shukaku Seal Bag) in the Shop closed the game. Now it says "Successfully purchased" and the card waits in the Awards chest (Zeraora). | in game |
| Browser game black | On Samsung (Mali) and PowerVR phones and in Firefox the browser game stayed black: the browser refused the game's drawing programs because of a precision mismatch in them. Fixed (Koolmanm598 and the page's own error reports). | Chrome; not yet on a Samsung phone |
