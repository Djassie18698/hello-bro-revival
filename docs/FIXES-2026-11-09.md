# Fixes of 9 November 2026

In plain words: what was wrong or new, what it does now and how it was checked.

Numbers marked **(ours)** are our own choice: the original value lived on the dead server and is lost.

---

## 1. Events rotate every week (ours)

The owner asked: "make events automatically rotate ... so that in a week you can see all the events. Leave Super Ninja still give a card
every 1 hour, but be there for 4 hours. I still want to be able to add or remove events, or reset rotation."

| Days | Events |
|---|---|
| all week | Diligence gift, Daily spending gift, Lottery, Recharge streak, Month card (bought month cards keep paying) |
| 1-2 | God of Wealth, Gold spending rewards, Time-limited GiftPack |
| 3-4 | Flower Worship, God Recruit, Limited group purchase (+ **Shard Rush** on day 4) |
| 5-7 | Hot Treasure, a festival (Christmas, New Year, Anniversary, Christmas 2nd year: a different one each week), Total-recharge gift (days 5-6) |
| 7 | God Recruit again, **Shard Rush** |
| every day | **Limited Super Ninja** 11:00-15:00 and 18:00-22:00 server time (UTC on the cloud: 12-16 and 19-23 in the Netherlands in winter); a new god card every hour; its cards step through all god cards one per hour of the windows, so every window shows the next ones |

Each time an event comes round it is a fresh run (its progress, stock and prizes start again), and its countdown in the game ends when
its days are over. Day 1 is the day the rotation started. **Server Manager > Weekly rotation**: switch it off (then every event follows
its own switch again), take an event out (it then follows its own switch) or put it back, and "Reset to day 1". The always-on features
(login rewards, Lucky Wheel, Rock paper scissors, chests ...) are not in it.

## 2. Every God card can break through: fragments from fights (ours)

Before: only 18 of the 83 God cards had any way to get fragments, so most could never break through. Now a won fight can give fragments
of **one of your own God cards** (one that is not at +10 yet):

| Fight won | Chance | Fragments |
|---|---|---|
| Training stage | 25 % | 1-2 |
| Boss stage | 60 % | 2-4 |
| Spar | 20 % | 1-2 |
| Arena | 30 % | 1-3 |

**Shard Rush** (days 4 and 7 of the rotation) doubles the fragments; the top banner says so at login. The owner changes all of these in
**Server Manager > God card shards**. The fragments land in Backpack > Fragments (the victory wheel itself does not show them). Good to
know: 50 fragments of a card also make a new copy of it (the game's own Synthesis). The wiki lists "Won fights (fragments)" on all 83 God cards.

Checked: on the test server (Event menu with the day-1 events, the GiftPack countdown ending with its 2 days, Super Ninja gone outside its
window, fragments after a training win in Backpack > Fragments) and by `tools/dev/smoke.js` (every event within a week, the Super Ninja
windows and its hourly cards, a new festival each week, take out / reset, countdowns, fragment drops, Shard Rush, +10 cards skipped, rate limits).
