# Fixes of 4 and 5 November 2026

What changed on 4 and 5 November, in plain words. It comes from Discord reports, your questions and a weekend test round:
Claude played a brand-new character on the BlueStacks instance **Claude** (level 1 to 10 by hand, then 25 and 30), checked the
public server's logs and every screen of its 9 players, and tried the two-player features on a test save.

**How it was checked:** "in game" = seen on the Claude emulator with a test server on its own port; "test save" = a throwaway database.

---

## 3. Tutorial: off for everyone (5 Nov)

The tutorial switch in the Server Manager never worked: the game starts its own tutorial for every new character, whatever the
server says. So since the 4 Nov update every new player got it, also on the public server, without the server's helpers (it could
get them stuck at level 5 or 10). You chose "leave it off": game update **28.0.35** turns it off inside the game, and the switch is
gone. Turning it back on is possible, but only for everybody at once. Checked in game: a new character starts with no tutorial.
