# Fixes of 6 November 2026

What changed on 6 November, in plain words. It comes from a test round of the late game: Claude put the test account at
level 100 (VIP 10, cards at the top level) on the BlueStacks instance **Claude** and went through every later screen:
Reincarnate, Team 2, Eight Gates, Secret shop, Battles tower, Country war, Country officials, Shinobi training, Evolve,
Akatsuki and the Cross-server battle.

**How it was checked:** "in game" = seen on the Claude emulator with a test server on its own port.

---

## 1. Cards and upgrades

| What | Detail | Checked |
|---|---|---|
| Cards stop at level 50 | In the original every card tops out at 50, and Reincarnate only lists cards at exactly 50. Upgrading could push a card to 51, and then Reincarnate said "No Lv.50 cards". Cards now stop at 50, and any card above it is put back to 50 when the server starts (one weapon on the public server). | in game: Karin reincarnated with 40 pills |
| Evolve checks the pair | The game only lets you pair an **attack** and a **defense** card of the same 5★ ninja. The server never checked, so a cheater could turn any two cards into a god card. Now it checks the same thing. | in game: Minato + Minato = god Minato |
| Evolve gives the training pills back | The confirm window says "the super ninja pills used will be returned". The server now counts the Super Ninja Pills spent training each card and gives them back on Evolve, with the game's own message. Only training done from now on is counted. | in game: 3 pills spent, 3 back |

## 2. Country war and officials

| What | Detail | Checked |
|---|---|---|
| War log | "get 10 points and **0** exploit" now says 10 exploit, which is what you really get. | in game |
| War hours | The Country tab said "war starts everynight 0:00-0:00". The war is open all day here (your choice), so it now says 0:00-24:00. | in game |
| Seat price | Competing for a seat was free. Now the original price the game asks for: Raikage 20 gold, Custodian 10, Elder free. | in game: Custodian -10 gold |
| Daily official gift | Now the game's own pack, so you get what the bag says: Raikage 5★ or 4★ card or 500 gold, Custodian the same with 300 gold, Elder 5★, 4★ or 3★ card or 100 gold. | in game: Custodian 300 gold |
