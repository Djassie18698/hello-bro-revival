The secret weapon Obito used after turning into Ten Tails' Jinchūriki. Equip it to [Obito] for a combo.

Where to get it

  • Story boss: Madara Revival: Sage Hengoku (5 % chance a win)
  • Fragments: Collect 30 of its fragments, then Backpack > Fragments > Synthesis
  • Shop gift bag: Shop > Gift bags: a 4-star weapon bag gives 3-5 stars
