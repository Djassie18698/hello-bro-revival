Despite a relatively low combat power, he had rare intellect that consistently allows him to prevail in combat. It's said he had an IQ of 200. Using the secret Ninjutsu from Nara clan, to employ the use of his shadow to strangle and attack.

Where to get it

  • Starter: One of the three cards you can pick when you make a character (Neji, Shikamaru or Sakura)
  • Purple recruit: Shop > Recruit (300 gold, free every 3 days)
  • Blue recruit: Shop > Recruit (150 gold, free every 2 days)
  • Recruitment Order: Shop > Recruit (one Recruitment Order a pull)
  • Story boss: New Hokage: Bring Sasuke (5 % chance a win)
  • Fragments: Collect 15 of its fragments, then Backpack > Fragments > Synthesis
