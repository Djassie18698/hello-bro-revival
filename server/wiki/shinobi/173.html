Onoki

Stats

Stubborn character with a temper, called Old Man by Kurotsuchi. For a word from Kazekage Gaara during the Five Kage summit, he changed his stubborn character to fully support the shinobi alliance. The only alive shinobi who ever fought with Madara Uchiha

Let the shields of dust spilt the enemy into atoms!

Chains

  • Fight with Uchiha Madara +25% DEF
  • Fight with Second Tsuchikage +25% DEF
  • Fight with Deidara +25% DEF
  • Fight with Gaara +25% DEF
  • Fight with Sasuke Uchiha +25% DEF

Where to get it

  • Purple recruit: Shop > Recruit (300 gold, free every 3 days)
  • Blue recruit: Shop > Recruit (150 gold, free every 2 days)
  • Recruitment Order: Shop > Recruit (one Recruitment Order a pull)
  • Mystery shop: Shop > Mystery shop, 5000 souls (it shows 6 random offers, new ones every few hours)
  • Mystery shop (1 fragment): Shop > Mystery shop, 170 souls (it shows 6 random offers, new ones every few hours)
  • Mystery shop (5 fragments): Shop > Mystery shop, 850 souls (it shows 6 random offers, new ones every few hours)
  • Fragments: Collect 30 of its fragments, then Backpack > Fragments > Synthesis
