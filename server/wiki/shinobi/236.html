One of the Seven Ninja Swordsmen of Kirigakure, and the only female of Seven Ninja Swordsmen. Carrier of Sword Kiba and fought with Thunder power. Known as the Raiton Genius and died when fighting for MizuKage.

The thunder knife "Kiba" has the power to split everything!
