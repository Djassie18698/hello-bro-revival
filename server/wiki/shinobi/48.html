Six-Tails Saiken Jinchūriki was Utakata of Kirigakure in Land of Water. He wore a long, light blue kimono, carried a bamboo jug filled with a soap solution. A handsome youth with long bangs. He was Missing-nin of Kirigakure and using Soap Bubble Ninjutsu.

Can you guess if these bubbles can kill you?
