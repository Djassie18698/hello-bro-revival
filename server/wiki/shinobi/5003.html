Kaguya, mother of the sage of six paths, resurrected by madara and zetsu, seeks revenge on the entire Shinobi world through the use of infinite tsukuyomi and her unimaginable powers over chakra.

Chains

  • Fight with Six Path Madara +25% DEF
  • Fight with Kyuubi Naruto,Susa Sasuke +25% DEF
