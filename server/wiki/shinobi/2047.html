Second Tsuchikage, entire body was covered by wrapped bandages. He trained Third Tsuchikage Ōnoki during his lifetime. Created Jinton through simultaneous use of the earth, wind, and fire natures. He's the Bloodline Elimination, beyond the Bloodline Limit

Nobody can discover me! Nobody can stop me!

Chains

  • Fight with Onoki +25% DEF and ATK
  • Fight with Third Raikage and Fourth Kazekage +20% ATK and DEF
  • Fight with Itachi Uchiha +30% DEF and ATK
  • Fight with Second Mizukage +20% DEF and ATK
  • Fight with Uchiha Madara +20% DEF and ATK
  • Raise all shinobis' ATK and DEF by 3%

Where to get it

  • God evolve: Home > evolve: the attack card and the defense card of Second Tsuchikage (five-star, not in your team) + 100 Development Dan; Super Ninja Pills used on them come back
  • Limited Super Ninja: Home > Super Ninja: every god card has its own hour; 1st place in that hour's points ranking wins it
  • Won fights (fragments): Won training stages, bosses, Spar and Arena fights can drop fragments of the god cards you own (double on Shard Rush days); they go to Breakthrough or, 50 of them, to a new copy
  • Fragments: Collect 50 of its fragments, then Backpack > Fragments > Synthesis
