Yakushi Kabuto

Nominally a shinobi of Konohagakure, actually a heeler of Orochimaru, sent to Konohagakure as a spy by Orochimaru. He was cunning and used as an important tool of Orochimaru. Medical Ninjutsu was his profession.

Allow reincarnation to control samsara!

Chains

  • Fight with Orochimaru +25% DEF
  • Fight with Itachi Uchiha +25% DEF
  • Fight with Uchiha Obito +25% DEF
  • Equip Ninjutsu Reincarnation +25% DEF
  • Fight with Hashirama Senju +25% DEF

Where to get it

  • Purple recruit: Shop > Recruit (300 gold, free every 3 days)
  • Blue recruit: Shop > Recruit (150 gold, free every 2 days)
  • Recruitment Order: Shop > Recruit (one Recruitment Order a pull)
  • Mystery shop (1 fragment): Shop > Mystery shop, 270 souls (it shows 6 random offers, new ones every few hours)
  • Mystery shop (3 fragments): Shop > Mystery shop, 810 souls (it shows 6 random offers, new ones every few hours)
  • Fragments: Collect 30 of its fragments, then Backpack > Fragments > Synthesis
