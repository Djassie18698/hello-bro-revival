Chieftain of Uchiha branch and founder of Konohagakure on Land of Fire. His vision power Eternal Mangekyō was able to control Tailed Beast, made him the most powerful shinobi in Uchiha history. He made to the top as Hashirama Senju. Defeated by Hashirama

This world is so small it is only enough for one person, that is me, to torment!

Chains

  • Fight with Uchiha Obito +25% DEF and ATK
  • Equip Eternal Mangekyō +30% ATK and DEF
  • Fight with Hashirama Senju +25% DEF and ATK
  • Equip Uchiwa of Fire +25% ATK and DEF
  • Top shinobi grant s all Shinobi +11% ATK and DEF
  • Raise all ATK and DEF values by 7%

Where to get it

  • God evolve: Home > evolve: the attack card and the defense card of Uchiha Madara (five-star, not in your team) + 100 Development Dan; Super Ninja Pills used on them come back
  • Limited Super Ninja: Home > Super Ninja: every god card has its own hour; 1st place in that hour's points ranking wins it
  • Won fights (fragments): Won training stages, bosses, Spar and Arena fights can drop fragments of the god cards you own (double on Shard Rush days); they go to Breakthrough or, 50 of them, to a new copy
  • Fragments: Collect 50 of its fragments, then Backpack > Fragments > Synthesis
