Gamabunta

The chief toad of Mount Myōboku, summoned by Jiraiya, Yondaime and Naruto Uzumaki, called Gama Boss by Naruto. He was the father of GamaKichi and Gamaron, son of Two Great Sage Toads.

Be prepared to receive the attack of the strongest spirit beast!

Chains

  • Fight with Fukasaku and Shima +25% ATK
  • Fight with Jiraiya +25% ATK
  • Fight with GamaKichi and Gamaron +25% ATK
  • Fight with Minato Namikaze and Naruto Uzumaki +25% ATK

Where to get it

  • Purple recruit: Shop > Recruit (300 gold, free every 3 days)
  • Blue recruit: Shop > Recruit (150 gold, free every 2 days)
  • Recruitment Order: Shop > Recruit (one Recruitment Order a pull)
  • Story boss: More power: Revenge (5 % chance a win)
  • Fragments: Collect 15 of its fragments, then Backpack > Fragments > Synthesis
