Naruto and Hinata(A)

5-star attack shinobi card

Stats

Stars5-star
TypeAttack
Attack at level 1480-620
Defence at level 1290-375

Each level addsATK 35-41, DEF 22-25, Chakra 26-29

After the War, Naruto and Hinata were often seen hanging out together. The two kiss as they are floating in the sky with a big round moon in the background. In the ending credit, everyone attends Naruto and Hinata's wedding.

Chains

  • Fight with Namikaze +25% ATK
  • Fight with Boruto +25% ATK
  • Fight with Tsunade +25% ATK
  • Fight with Madara +25% ATK
  • Raise all shinobis +11% ATK and DEF

Where to get it

  • Mystery shop (1 fragment): Shop > Mystery shop, 400 souls (it shows 6 random offers, new ones every few hours)
  • Fragments: Collect 30 of its fragments, then Backpack > Fragments > Synthesis
