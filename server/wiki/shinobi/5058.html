MinaKushi is the term used to refer to the romantic relationship between Minato Namikaze and Kushina Uzumaki. They are one of the canon couples in the series and are the parents of Naruto Uzumaki.

Chains

  • Fight with Hashirama +25% ATK and DEF
  • Fight with Kakashi +25% ATK and DEF
  • Fight with Obito +25% ATK and DEF
  • Fight with Kai +25% ATK and DEF
  • All Shinobis +13% ATK and DEF
  • Raise all shinobis +7% ATK and DEF

Where to get it

  • God evolve: Home > evolve: the attack card and the defense card of Namikaze and Kushina (five-star, not in your team) + 100 Development Dan; Super Ninja Pills used on them come back
  • Limited Super Ninja: Home > Super Ninja: every god card has its own hour; 1st place in that hour's points ranking wins it
  • Ninja Test shop (15 fragments): Base > Ninja Test > score shop, 100 score (only when you own this god card)
  • Won fights (fragments): Won training stages, bosses, Spar and Arena fights can drop fragments of the god cards you own (double on Shard Rush days); they go to Breakthrough or, 50 of them, to a new copy
  • Fragments: Collect 50 of its fragments, then Backpack > Fragments > Synthesis
