Jashin Hidan (God)

God balanced shinobi card

Stats

StarsGod
TypeBalanced
Attack at level 1480-620
Defence at level 1480-620
Chakra at level 1480-620
Each level addsATK 35-41, DEF 35-41, Chakra 35-41

Hidan changes appearance after ingesting his opponent's blood. His skin then turns black with white markings representing a skeleton, a symbol of absolute death. He draws Jashin's symbol on the ground in his own blood and stands in the centre.

Chains

  • Fight with Itachi +25% ATK and DEF
  • Fight with Nagato +25% ATK and DEF
  • Fight with Kakashi +25% ATK and DEF
  • Equip Triple-Bladed Scythe +30% ATK and DEF
  • All shinobi +11% ATK and DEF
  • Raise all shinobi's 7% ATK and DEF

Where to get it

  • God evolve: Home > evolve: the attack card and the defense card of Jashin Hidan (five-star, not in your team) + 100 Development Dan; Super Ninja Pills used on them come back
  • Limited Super Ninja: Home > Super Ninja: every god card has its own hour; 1st place in that hour's points ranking wins it
  • Ninja Test shop (15 fragments): Base > Ninja Test > score shop, 100 score (only when you own this god card)
  • Won fights (fragments): Won training stages, bosses, Spar and Arena fights can drop fragments of the god cards you own (double on Shard Rush days); they go to Breakthrough or, 50 of them, to a new copy
  • Fragments: Collect 50 of its fragments, then Backpack > Fragments > Synthesis
