Kaguya for New Year(God)

God balanced shinobi card

Stats

StarsGod

New year new card,the people who own Kaguya for new year will receive power and happiness.

Chains

  • Fight with Six Path Sage +35% ATK and DEF
  • Fight with Susa Kakashi +30% ATK and DEF
  • Fight with Ōtsutsuki Hamura +30% ATK and DEF
  • Equip The Six Path Rinnegan +30% ATK and DEF
  • All Shinobi +16% ATK and DEF
  • Raise all Shinobi +10% ATK and DEF

Where to get it

  • Festival (5 fragments): Happy New Year event trade: 100 Christmas Cap + 100 Christmas Tree
  • Festival: Happy New Year event trade: 350 Christmas Cap + 350 Christmas Tree
  • God evolve: Home > evolve: the attack card and the defense card of Kaguya for New Year (five-star, not in your team) + 100 Development Dan; Super Ninja Pills used on them come back
  • Limited Super Ninja: Home > Super Ninja: every god card has its own hour; 1st place in that hour's points ranking wins it
  • God Recruit: The God Recruit event wheel
  • Ninja Test shop (15 fragments): Base > Ninja Test > score shop, 100 score (only when you own this god card)
  • Won fights (fragments): Won training stages, bosses, Spar and Arena fights can drop fragments of the god cards you own (double on Shard Rush days); they go to Breakthrough or, 50 of them, to a new copy
  • Fragments: Collect 50 of its fragments, then Backpack > Fragments > Synthesis
