Hokage Naruto (God)

God balanced shinobi card

Stats

StarsGod
TypeBalanced
Attack at level 1600-850
Defence at level 1600-850
Chakra at level 1600-850
Each level addsATK 43-54, DEF 43-54, Chakra 43-54

Naruto worked hard to gain the village's acknowledgement all the while chasing his dream to become Hokage. After Fourth Shinobi World War, leading him to achieve his dream and become the village's Seventh Hokage.

Chains

  • Fight with Kaguya +30% ATK and DEF
  • Fight with Sage Six Path +30% ATK and DEF
  • Fight with Madara +30% ATK and DEF
  • Fight with Sasuke +35% ATK and DEF
  • Raise all shinobi's 18% ATK and DEF
  • All Shinobi +13% ATK and DEF

Where to get it

  • Limited Super Ninja: Home > Super Ninja: every god card has its own hour; 1st place in that hour's points ranking wins it
  • Ninja Test shop (15 fragments): Base > Ninja Test > score shop, 100 score (only when you own this god card)
  • Won fights (fragments): Won training stages, bosses, Spar and Arena fights can drop fragments of the god cards you own (double on Shard Rush days); they go to Breakthrough or, 50 of them, to a new copy
  • Fragments: Collect 50 of its fragments, then Backpack > Fragments > Synthesis
