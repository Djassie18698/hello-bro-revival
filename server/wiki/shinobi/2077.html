Asura (God)

God balanced shinobi card

Stats

StarsGod
TypeBalanced
Attack at level 1455-578
Defence at level 1455-578
Chakra at level 1455-578
Each level addsATK 33-41, DEF 33-41, Chakra 33-41

The second son of six path,Indra's brother, He holds the power of love.

Wish the world to give up hatred and cruelty!

Chains

  • Fight with Six Paths +25% ATK and DEF
  • Fight with Indra +25% ATK and DEF
  • Fight with Hashirama +25% ATK and DEF
  • Fight with Naruto +25% ATK and DEF
  • All Shinobi get 10% ATK and DEF
  • Raise all Shinobi 5% ATK and DEF

Where to get it

  • God evolve: Home > evolve: the attack card and the defense card of Asura (five-star, not in your team) + 100 Development Dan; Super Ninja Pills used on them come back
  • Limited Super Ninja: Home > Super Ninja: every god card has its own hour; 1st place in that hour's points ranking wins it
  • Won fights (fragments): Won training stages, bosses, Spar and Arena fights can drop fragments of the god cards you own (double on Shard Rush days); they go to Breakthrough or, 50 of them, to a new copy
  • Fragments: Collect 50 of its fragments, then Backpack > Fragments > Synthesis
