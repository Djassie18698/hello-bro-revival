Ninja Online, the 2015 ninja card game

Ninja Online, also known as Hello Bro, is a ninja card RPG for Android that came out in 2015. Its servers went offline years ago. Now it runs again on a server rebuilt by its players.

Download it Open the wiki

What you do in the game

  • Collect shinobi. 493 cards, from 1 star up to God cards. Level them up, give them gear, seals and ninjutsu.
  • Play the story. 19 chapters and 1 234 stages, from the Shinobi Exams to the final war, and every boss brings its own team.
  • Fight other players in the Arena and the Country War, and plunder seals.
  • Join an Akatsuki guild with friends and take on the world boss together.
  • Grow stronger with Breakthrough, the Eight Gates, pets, rebirth and sage growth.

Every card, piece of gear and stage is in the wiki, and the team calculator works out your team's attack.

One game, more than one name

Most players know it as Hello Bro or Ninja Online. It is the same app: the package name is com.hello.bro.yo.

Play it now

It is free and works on Android phones and in BlueStacks on a PC. Download the game file on the download page, or read how the server works.
